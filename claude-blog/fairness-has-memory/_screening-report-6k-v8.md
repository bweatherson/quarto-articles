# Screening report v8: "Fairness Has Memory" (6k version)

**Paper:** Fairness Has Memory (`fairness-has-memory-6k.qmd`, build of 26 Oct 2026)
**Word count:** author's count 5,896; my count 5,941 (abstract + body + footnotes + table, citation keys stripped). At the limit: every addition below is paid for by a named cut.
**Thesis:** Within Broome's theory of fairness, a sequence of indivisible units allocated among a persistent pool is a divisible good, so a sequence of individually fair lotteries can be an unfair allocation (non-separability) and fairness requires that chances in later rounds track each claimant's shortfall from her share (memory). How hard memory may press is fixed by weighing the stayers' dispersion against the expected share owed to those who leave early, in Broome's own manner: where the good recurs, memory is required and the points and queue rules in use are dominated; where winners leave and some leave unserved (bottlenecks), the lottery that forgets is what fairness requires.

**Verdict: NOT READY**

The recurrence half of the paper is sound and I checked it line by line: the derivations in §5.2 hold, every number in Table 1 and the prose reproduces, and the dominance claims hold on the table's measures and at two other churn rates I tried. The paper fails on the other half. The bottleneck verdict is the paper's advertised reversal (abstract, §1, §8) and it is not derived; §5.3 builds nothing with the method §5.2 built and decides by "I judge." That is the fourth appearance of the same fault, relocated: the lexical floor has been removed from recurrence and survives, unnamed, in the bottleneck.

## Deal-breakers

**Criterion 4 / "could the author have seen it": the bottleneck verdict is asserted, not weighed, and is reported upstream as a result.** §5.2 establishes the method: both the leavers' expected share and the stayers' dispersion are "departures from proportional satisfaction," they are put in one currency (units of the good), the frontier is computed (Table 1), and a corner (forgetting) is rejected because the exchange is "lopsided" by orders of magnitude, with the explicit concession that "the theory fixes the frontier rather than the point on it." §5.3 then announces a corner with no frontier, no numbers and no currency: "I judge the second weightier than the first, and so … fairness requires the lottery that forgets." The abstract and §8 report this as "the same weighing forbids memory" and "it is not at all." It is not the same weighing. In recurrence both sides were in units of the good; in a bottleneck one side is a chance of a unit and the other is periods of waiting, and the exchange rate between them is exactly what the paper's own Fungibility concept (§3: "a matter of degree") leaves open and makes case-dependent. The paper classes bottlenecks as the cases where the claim is "largely to timeliness," which is a reason to weight waiting, and then rules that waiting may not be weighted, without saying what became of that claim.

I ran the §5.2 method on the bottleneck (pool of 10 kept full, one unit per period, unserved attrition 1% per period, 200,000 periods): the queue gives a newcomer 0 and a waiting SD of 0.6 periods; forgetting gives 1.000 and 8.7; a mild weight on waiting (chance ∝ 1 + 0.1·wait) gives 0.61 and 6.6, and cuts the share of served claimants who wait 40+ periods from 1.0% to 0.04%. So the paper's structural point is right and strong: with no past winner's surplus to draw on, even a mild memory costs a newcomer two-fifths of her chance, where κ = 1 in recurrence cost 0.7%. But that supports "memory is far costlier here," not "forbidden." Under a linear weighing, forgetting beats every alternative only if a newcomer's first-period chance (in multiples of 1/N) is worth more than about eight periods of waiting-SD; below that the queue wins. Nothing in the paper says where that rate lies or why. Two further things the author could have seen: (i) the fraction who leave unserved is the same under every rule (conservation; 0.917 in every run), so forgetting does not give "those who will leave unserved" the good, it changes who they are, which the paper's own §7 ("Dated at her entry, every rule gives her the same prospects") already implies and §5.3 obscures; (ii) in §5.2 the paper declines to forbid κ = 10, a 48% leak, calling it "a real exchange," while in §5.3 "any rule that weights by time waited" is forbidden, at leaks of 40% and less. Those two verdicts need one principle, and the paper has none.

Fixing this is not three sentences of hedging. The abstract's last clause, §1's "the structure in which it is forbidden," §5.3's verdict paragraph and §8's second sentence all have to say the weaker thing the argument supports, and §5.3 has to show the frontier it is judging on.

## Key strengths

- The divisibility argument is genuinely inside Broome's premises and the cake reductio makes the non-separability result vivid; the three-structure reply to the extinction thesis (re-run, interrupted allocation, recurrence) is the right dissection and correctly places Vong and Feldblyum Le Blevennec.
- The formal core is correct. I derived (i) conservation, (ii) exactness of the shortfall rule absent clipping (E[d] shrinks by 1 − κ/N with no constant term because the pool mean of standing is invariant and a random exit does not shift it in expectation), and (iii) both closed forms: V = (N−1)/(2κN) fixed, and (N−1)/(N(Nq + 2κ(1−q))) at exit via E[b^T] for geometric tenure; the "units per stay" reading 1/(1 + 2κ(1−q)·(1/Nq)) follows.
- The dominance result against real institutional rules is the paper's most transferable finding and it is robust: Arizona, Nevada, Grand Canyon dominated at q = 0.1, 0.3 and 0.02; the queue dominated at all three (by κ = 10 at 0.1 and 0.02, by κ ≥ 30 at 0.3).
- The surplus asymmetry (memory in recurrence is paid by past winners; in a bottleneck by the never-served) is real and quantifiable; it is the paper's best idea about bottlenecks and deserves numbers rather than a verdict.

## Key weaknesses

- The surrogate constraint is stated one-sidedly ("fall short of proportionality") but applied two-sidedly: the Grand Canyon rule is condemned for giving a newcomer "a sixth more than her share," which the stated constraint does not forbid. §5.2's "both are departures from proportional satisfaction" is the right formulation; the constraint sentence contradicts it.
- "Dominated on both measures" is true for the two headline measures but not cell by cell: at tenure 10 Colorado's queue gives 1.006 ± .001 and κ = 10 gives 0.972 ± .003, and κ = 10 denies tenure-1 to -3 classes heavily (my run: 0.50, 0.71, 0.89). "For each there is a shortfall rule that does better by the leavers" needs "by the newcomer" or a clause.
- The normative premise carrying the third thesis, "If the allocator knew who would leave, it would owe them the surrogate outright … Not knowing, it owes the surrogate to everyone, in the only form available," is two sentences and is never argued against the obvious alternative (that an allocator who knew could simply serve the short-stayer, the long-stayers being made whole by later division). A referee will go straight for it.
- Broome's own suggestion to Vong (Utilitas 2015: 481, reported in Feldblyum Le Blevennec 2023) of weighted diachronic lotteries "adjusting the probabilities of subsequent lotteries" is a history-dependent lottery proposed by Broome himself; the paper's "against his own extinction thesis" framing should locate itself relative to it in a clause.

## Revision items (requested by the brief)

1. **§5.3 verdict paragraph.** Quote: "I judge the second weightier than the first, and so the result is two-sided a second way. Where everyone will be served … fairness requires a queue; where some will leave unserved and the allocator cannot tell who, fairness requires the lottery that forgets." Wrong: a corner chosen with no frontier, no currency and no exchange rate, inconsistent with §5.2's treatment of κ = 10. Fix (about +70 words): give the bottleneck frontier in one sentence (queue 0 / 0.6; mild weight 0.61 / 6.6; forgetting 1.00 / 8.7, attrition 1%), state that the fraction left unserved is the same under every rule so the choice is over who bears it, say that the exchange rate between a unit and a period of waiting is the degree to which Fungibility fails, and conclude: the queue is forbidden wherever exit is unforeseeable, since it denies every short exit-class the surrogate entirely; between forgetting and a mild weight the theory fixes the frontier, which is steep because no surplus pays; I judge forgetting, and say that this is a judgement. Pay for it by cutting §6 "A boundary question follows. *Transition*: … would count only the losses." (−40) and §7 "Memory does raise the value of a manufactured claim … fairness has no memory."
