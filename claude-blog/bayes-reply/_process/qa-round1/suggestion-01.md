**Your dominance standard also convicts your conservative rules. Across two-sided learning, any rule that gives the rim credence is dominated, so "the domain of evaluation" endorses only certainty that you are not at the edge. That is the verdict Hi-TEA was built to block.**

Run the §3 proof on an arbitrary rule *g*. Under the log score, *g*(· | int *p*) beats *g* at every interior state whenever *g* gives the rim positive credence. Under Brier, projecting *g*(*p*) onto the interior does the same. So your conservative mixtures are strictly dominated by the pure rules on every two-sided problem with a rim. Your Dana numbers show it: the mixture scores −0.09 and the pure rule 0. (Checked numerically; no exceptions.) Over the two-sided class, the undominated rules are exactly those that give the rim zero credence.

For the agents §5 invokes, who "know how they learn", it gets worse. Olivia learns each proposition at only one state, so over her possibilities only certainty of the truth is undominated. *f*<sub>log</sub> itself is dominated there: it gives credence 1/2 at 16 and at 19.

Now the defence. Meehan and Zhang raised your domain objection themselves: "by stipulation, Dana knows her learning situation" (p. 333). They filed it under weights (p. 335). Their challenge was "why BCOND and not the truth rule?" Your domain principle answers that the truth rule is right for anyone who knows her margins. You concede in "Absurdity" that this is implausible. The defender agrees. A principle that licenses only certainty about where the truth lies is a reductio of evaluating rules over one learner's possibilities. That is why accuracy is assessed "in general … irrespective of their uncertainties about their evidential situations" (n. 35). The same point disposes of "Impartiality is a compromise". The "sure loss" you charge to conditionalization also falls on every non-absurd rule you offer.

The escape has a price; name it. Hi-TEA must call rational a rule that Olivia can see is dominated by a globally evidentially constant rule open to her. Its accuracy is no one's in particular.
