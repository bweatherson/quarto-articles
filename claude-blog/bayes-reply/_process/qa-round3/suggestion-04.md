**Repair five places where the draft misreports Meehan and Zhang, and credit three concessions of theirs that it omits.**

I checked every MZ quotation and page reference. Nearly all are exact, including Table 1 (−2.25, −2.08, recomputed), Facts 4–7, 10, 11, and nn. 4, 6, 21, 29, 35. The problems:

1. **§5, "Their own sequence" (pp. 320–321, n. 30).** A credence that is "a function of the conjunction" of {15,16} and {15,16} would leave Dana certain, so the sentence contradicts itself. Nor is it MZ's sequence. On p. 321 it is left hand then right: one-fourth, then certain. In n. 30 (right then left) meta-conditionalization stays certain. Your view reproduces the p. 321 pattern; use that order.

2. **§5, "the point they call 'close to the bedrock'" (p. 337).** They say the dispute has reached bedrock, not that extensionality is bedrock. They grant that a non-Bayesian weighting by "how likely it is for one to learn *p* at *s*" gets a different optimum, "a fact that we have to live with rather than explain away" (pp. 337–338). Their Fact 12 (p. 338) is your steps 1–3 for a single situation, and they add that extensionality "is compatible with evidence externalism as well." Cite both, and temper footnote 1's novelty claim. What is new: for margin learners that weighting is forced.

3. **The headline claim against nn. 10–11 (p. 296) and n. 24 (p. 317).** MZ say an externalist "can get around the negative results … by rejecting strict propriety". They call their route "much less amenable to the accuracy-first project" and seek a "substantive connection" (p. 287), not reduction. Your Proposition needs strict propriety. Write "for strictly proper scores" in the abstract and §7, and call Hi-TEA a bridge principle.

4. **Remark 1 (pp. 305–306).** The only luminous rules in Olivia and Dana are constant rules such as the dogmatic rule, so "no more luminous than its rivals" overgeneralizes. Say "than the dominating rule". Margin covers only rules "no more sensitive than" and δ-close to a followable rule, and *f*<sub>*A*</sub> is not close. Apply it to the §6 mixtures.

5. **§2, "the kind that generates the opacity Meehan and Zhang address".** MZ call evidential constancy "neutral" about when one learns the same proposition and cite guess-based accounts (p. 317). Olivia is a paradigm, not their scope.

Smaller: "By stipulation" (p. 333) needs "[b]y". *ln c*(*s*) is MZ's "simple nonadditive" log score (n. 14, p. 302), so "Both are additive" misleads. Lemma 1 (p. 344) is stated for weights ω. "Radical response" (p. 334) names no one; Gallow and others are at pp. 288, 307.

Cut Remark 2 (about 40 words) to pay for items 2 and 3.
