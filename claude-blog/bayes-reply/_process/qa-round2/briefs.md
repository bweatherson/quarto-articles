01 (opus) THE ACCURACY-FIRST DEFENDER. You work in accuracy-first epistemology and think "Bayes Is Back" is excellent. You came to defend Meehan and Zhang. You are combative and technical. Find the weakest step in the paper's case against them, especially §5 (the slogan reply, the veil dilemma) and §6 (the claim that accuracy pushes the externalist toward credences she cannot have). Say how a sophisticated defender of Hi-TEA escapes and at what cost.

02 (opus) THE MATHEMATICIAN. You are a probabilist who works on proper scoring rules and Bregman divergences. Check the Proposition and its proof in §3 (including the use of Predd et al. 2009, the footnote on unbounded scores, the Brier projection description), remark 4's claim about uniformity and every weighting, the "Graded margins" subsection, the three steps and the table in §4, and the concavity argument in §6. Find anything false, unproved or misattributed. A copy of Predd et al. is at /mnt/user-data/outputs/bayes-reply/research/texts/oa/predd2009.txt.

03 (opus) THE WILLIAMSONIAN. You are a knowledge-first externalist (E = K, margins for error, evidential probability). Scrutinize §2's account of margins and slack, §6's argument that a two-sided knower cannot follow the dominating rules and stay two-sided, and §7's first option. Is §6 sound by Williamson's lights (safety, the margin principle, what certainty requires)? Is the first option fairly described?

Check every representation of Meehan and Zhang in the draft against the text: pp. 288, 294, 300–302, 304, 306, 309, 311 n. 21, 316, 319–321, 324, 327, 330, 333–334, 337–338, 344–345. 05 (opus) THE SIGNAL THEORIST. You work in the tradition of Gallow, Isaacs and Russell, and Easwaran and Nielsen, modelling updating as a response to signals or guesses with likelihoods. Check how the paper uses that work, especially the "Graded margins" subsection, the uniform-error clocks in §4 and the second option of §7. Is that option correctly characterized, as conditionalization on a tracked signal with slack-free margins? Is anything in the paper already in that literature without credit, or misdescribed? The relevant open texts are en2025.txt (Easwaran & Nielsen) and, for Isaacs and Russell and Gallow, the reports in /mnt/user-data/outputs/bayes-reply/process/canon-check.md.
