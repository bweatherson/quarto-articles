# Screening Report

**Paper:** A Counterexample to Arrhenius's Sixth Impossibility Theorem
**Word count:** c. 6,900 (body); 30 bibliography entries
**Thesis:** Arrhenius's sixth impossibility theorem (no axiology satisfies ED, GNEP, NE, WNS and WQA) is false as stated: a complete, transitive "capacity view" on Arrhenius's own integer welfare levels, which ranks populations first by min(0, #excellent lives + total negative welfare) and then by total welfare, satisfies all five conditions in their exact formulations while accepting the Repugnant Conclusion and rejecting the Very Repugnant Conclusion. The Thomas/Thornley strengthening of WQA (fix the quantity of misery before the background) is therefore necessary, not a cosmetic repair, and what it rules out is exactly axiologies on which the quantity of misery that marginal lives cannot redeem depends on how much excellence the background contains.

**Decision: EVALUATE**

The central argument is axiomatic ethics with a formal core (an impossibility theorem, a countermodel, a machine-checked proof), which is a listed EVALUATE trigger. The fuller assessment below is mine; what follows is where I came out.

## Key Strengths

- **The central claim is correct, and I checked it independently.** I re-derived Non-Elitism with all cases (x−1 ≥ A; x < A split at x ≥ 1, x = 0, x < 0; and x = A split on the sign of y) and Weak Quality Addition, and also ED, GNEP and WNS. Every case goes through; the one place I(P) can fall under a licensed transfer (NE with x = A, y ≥ 0) is correctly handled by the restriction D ⊂ R(y, A), which forces J = 0 on both sides. The witnesses satisfy Arrhenius's exact formulations (lines 333–394 of the 2011 chapter), including the implicit y ≥ 3 from "ranges contain at least three levels," which the author respects by choosing y = 3. Nothing is quietly weakened: the "who chooses what" order in WQA ("for any X there are x, u, y, n, m") is reproduced exactly, and the Lean `WeakQualityAddition` in Conditions.lean matches it quantifier for quantifier. The diagnosis of Arrhenius's error is also right: step (12) of Lemma 1.3 (p. 18–19) applies WQA to A₁ ∪ X with witnesses supplied for X.
- **This is a genuine result, not a reply.** Thomas said the premisses "have to" be strengthened; Thornley adopted the strengthening and called it harmless. Neither showed the original theorem false. A countermodel inside Arrhenius's own framework (integer levels, Discreteness intact) is new, and the §4 distinction from lexical-welfare escapes (Lexical Totalism, which denies Finite Fine-Grainedness) is correctly drawn: here the lexicality is in the ranking of populations, not of lives.
- **Generalist appeal is real.** The Repugnant Conclusion and Arrhenius's theorems are among the best-known results in ethics; a philosopher outside population ethics would care that the favoured theorem fails, and the closing methodological point (quantifier-dependence errors in page-long inequality chains survive refereeing; proof assistants catch them) travels beyond the subfield.
- **Proposition 2 is correct** and its consequence is worth having: for any axiology satisfying Compensation, WQA (and WQA*) collapses to Quality Addition, so the sixth theorem's advertised advance over the fourth has content only against non-Archimedean views. That is a clean clarification of what the theorem was ever doing.

## Key Weaknesses

- **§4's rhetorical payoff is partly muddled.** The paper says "WQA* is not a weakening of the earlier Quality conditions in the way WQA was advertised to be; for every axiology on which misery can be compensated by marginal lives, it is equivalent to Quality Addition." But Proposition 2 applies identically to WQA, so this cannot be what distinguishes WQA* from WQA; the only real difference is the background-independence clause, and the paragraph conflates the two. Relatedly, the "iff" in "accepts Weak Quality Addition if and only if it accepts Quality Addition" is only half-proved: Prop 2 gives WQA ⇒ QA; QA ⇒ WQA needs that adding bad lives never helps, which is assumed, not stated.
- **The normative question is raised and then left.** "Is that structure one an adequacy condition should rule out? I do not think the question has an obvious answer." The paper's defence that the structural idea is "not absurd" is one paragraph of assertion. It does not confront the view's sharpest consequence, which follows immediately from the definition: a billion lives at level A−1 plus one life at −1 has J = −1 and is therefore *worse than the empty population*. The author knows the view is lexical ("a single unit of uncovered misery outweighs any quantity of total welfare") but never states the implication in the form a reader will, and the "not absurd" claim has to survive it.
- **"The repaired theorem is true" is overstated relative to what is verified.** T6star in Theorems.lean uses Thomas's fixed-threshold conditions (GNEP with A fixed and single-level low populations, WNS with Z fixed). §6 ¶3 concedes this, but the abstract and §4 assert the repaired theorem flatly, and §6 also misdescribes Thomas's conditions as uniformly "stronger" than Arrhenius's (Thomas's GNEP demands less in one respect, more in another). The exact-formulation repaired theorem is very probably true but is not what was checked.
- **§5 (the method) is a sketch with unverifiable claims.** No solver code accompanies the paper; "it delivered the capacity view in the first run" and the infeasibility claims for the fifth theorem "for every small witness size" cannot be checked. The stated rationale for the cap at zero ("ED is strict and a bounded lexical layer cannot carry strict comparisons") is not the actual reason; the uncapped Φ = H + T⁻ passes ED and fails only NE at x = A, which the paper separately calls "the one subtlety."
- **Minor fidelity point in the Lean conditions.** The formal statements omit the "at least three levels" constraint on ranges (they require only 0 < y), and WNS adds n > 0 where Arrhenius does not. Both are harmless to the countermodel (the witnesses used satisfy the stricter reading; adding n > 0 strengthens the condition), but a paper whose selling point is exactness should say so.

## Critical Issues for Revision

1. Rewrite the paragraph in §4 beginning "So the corrected sixth theorem is a theorem about background-relative repugnance" so that the WQA/WQA* contrast rests solely on background-dependence, and either prove QA ⇒ WQA under an explicit no-sadism assumption or drop the "iff."
2. State the empty-population implication of the capacity view explicitly and say whether the "misery is redeemable by excellence, not by numbers" idea survives it; if the answer is that it does not, say so and reframe the §4 conclusion as a result about what WQA* excludes rather than a half-defence of capacity views.
3. Either formalize the repaired theorem for Arrhenius's exact (existentially quantified threshold) conditions, or qualify every statement of "the repaired theorem is true" as Thomas's fixed-threshold version, and correct the "weaker than Thomas's versions" sentence in §6.
4. Supply the LP formulation and solver script for §5, or cut §5 to the two paragraphs that are argued in the text (the linear-inequality translation and the diagnosis of why Theorems 1–5 resist the method) and remove the unverifiable claims about the search history.
5. Add the ≥ 3-levels range constraint to the Lean definitions (or note the omission and why it is harmless) so the "exact formulations" claim is literally true of the checked statements.
