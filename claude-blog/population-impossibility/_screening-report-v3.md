# Screening Report

**Paper:** A Counterexample to Arrhenius's Sixth Impossibility Theorem
**Word count:** c. 7,900 (body), 30-item bibliography
**Thesis:** Arrhenius's sixth impossibility theorem is false as stated: the "capacity view" (rank populations lexically by min(0, excellent lives + negative welfare), then by total welfare) satisfies all five conditions in Arrhenius's exact 2011 formulations on integer welfare levels, accepting the Repugnant Conclusion while rejecting the Very Repugnant one. The Thomas/Thornley repair (quantifier swap in Weak Quality Addition) is true but not free: it rules out, by stipulation, views on which the uncompensable quantity of misery depends on the background, and the four remaining conditions force only a background-relative repugnance. Everything is accompanied by a Lean 4 development and an LP-based method for finding such countermodels.

**Decision: EVALUATE** (axiomatic ethics; squarely in the editor's formal area). My fuller assessment is below; on it the paper clears the bar for external review once the overclaims listed under Revision are removed. None of the problems I found is decisive against the thesis.

## What I checked

- **§3, Theorem 1.** I re-derived all five conditions by hand. ED: ΔT > 0 and ΔI ≥ 0 in all three cases for the sign of x. GNEP with (u, y, n) = (A, 3, 2): ΔI ≥ 1 (the two "−1" cases z+1 = A and z+1 ≤ 0 are exclusive), ΔT ≥ 2A − 7 > 0. NE with n = 1: the only I-lowering case is x = A, y ≥ 0, where the restriction D ⊂ R(y, A) guarantees both sides have J = 0; this is exactly where General Non-Elitism (Theorem 5) would break the view, as the paper says. WNS and WQA are immediate. The conditions in §2 match Arrhenius 2011 pp. 8–9 verbatim in distribution notation; the Lean statements in `Conditions.lean` match them, and the three recorded deviations (y ≥ 3 for ranges, n > 0 in ED, n > 0 in WNS for the countermodel only) all go in the safe direction for a countermodel. No quiet weakening. The diagnosis of Arrhenius's step (12) in Lemma 1.3 (WQA witnesses for X used against background A₁ ∪ X) is correct against the text.
- **Lean.** `lake build` succeeds (Lean 4.34.1, core only), 0 `sorry`, 1,189 lines; `#print axioms` on `sixth_theorem_countermodel`, `T1`–`T5`, `T6star`, `T6star_exact` gives only propext/choice/Quot.sound, as claimed. Thomas's hypothesis sets (T4: ED, GNEP, NE, WNS → RA; T5: DA, ED, GNE, GNEP → VRC; T6*: ED, GNEP, NE, WNS → VRA*) match his note. The §6 description of `T6star_exact` (common high level, extra IAA merge step) matches the code.
- **§4.** Proposition 2 is correct (Compensation applied to X + B, then WQA on B + K·3 ⊂ R(1,y), then transitivity), and the "same argument for WQA*" remark is right. Corollary 3 is correctly derived from Prop 2 plus Thomas's T4 and the fixed-threshold restriction is explicitly stated and legitimate (Prop 2 goes through in Thomas's vocabulary; RA's strict conclusion is contradicted by the weak ≥ that Prop 2 delivers). The VRA/VRA* quotations are verbatim from Thomas (p. 11). The logical remark that ¬WQA* is weaker than VRA* for incomplete orderings is right; the stated equivalence for complete orderings is only exact when both are in Thomas's fixed-threshold vocabulary, not between Thomas's VRA* and the exact WQA* as printed. Lexical Totalism is correctly attributed (Thornley's "very negative iff h ≤ f < 0" and "barely positive only if h = 0" do give misery + marginal lives h < 0, worse than the empty population), but the quotation is not verbatim: Thornley writes "adding a combination of very negative welfare lives and barely positive welfare lives means adding (r, s) with r < 0". The graded-view remark is honest (numerical only; the Lean proof is for the step version) and my own check of the transfer inequalities for A = 4..10 confirms it.
- **§5.** The script runs and its output matches the text exactly (T6 feasible at witness 1 with w = ℓ below 0, 0 on 0..3, 1 at ≥ A; T1–T5 infeasible). The diagnosis for T1–T4 (refusing a misery-free conclusion needs w₃ < 0; the cap needs w₃ ≥ 0) is correct and is literally how the script fails. I also confirmed the T5 diagnosis: ED+GNE+GNEP alone is infeasible while ED+DA+NE+GNEP is feasible, so GNE really is the culprit. But the paper misdescribes T5's premises as "replace Non-Elitism by General Non-Elitism"; Thomas's T5 also replaces WNS with Dominance Addition.

## Key Strengths

- The central result is new, correct, and verifiable: Thomas (2016, 2018) and Thornley (2021) said the proof has a gap and the premise should be strengthened; nobody showed the original theorem false. The capacity view does so on Arrhenius's own integer levels, i.e. without the denial of Finite Fine-Grainedness that every previous counterexample (Lexical Totalism) relies on. §4's scope remark gets this contrast exactly right.
- Proposition 2 / Corollary 3 are a genuine conceptual payoff: for any Archimedean (Compensation-satisfying) view, WQA, WQA* and Quality Addition coincide, so the weakening that distinguishes the sixth theorem from the fourth has content only against Compensation-violating views, and given the other four conditions WQA entails that Compensation fails. That sharpens what the sixth theorem is actually about.
- The LP method (§5) is a transferable idea with an honest limits paragraph, and its account of *why* the sixth theorem is the only one of the six with a gap (the only misery-involving conclusion whose transfer conditions do not close it) is correct and explanatory.
- The Lean development is real, self-contained, and does the one thing a referee cannot: certify that a verified countermodel and a verified repaired theorem are not in tension (§6, last paragraph).

## Key Weaknesses

- The philosophical centre of §4, that VRA* is "the Repugnant Conclusion again" and so not newly repugnant, is argued from inside the capacity view's own gloss ("the background redeems the misery"). A critic of the VRC will say VRA*'s verdict (in some sufficiently good world, adding tortured lives plus enough marginal lives beats adding excellent lives) is just what they find repugnant, however the background is described. The paper hedges ("I do not think the question has an obvious answer") but does not engage the objection in that form.
- The paper dwells (§3, §4) on the view's "sharpest consequence", that a billion lives at level 3 plus one at −1 is worse than nothing, and defends it as shared with Lexical Totalism. But the consequence is an artefact of the empty background having zero capacity: replacing I by I + c for a constant c ≥ 1 removes it while preserving all five conditions (every ΔI argument is unaffected; WQA's m just grows by c; the view still rejects VRC for m|z| > c). The author made exactly this "artefact of the simplest potential" move for the threshold at A but not here, and even notes that "Compensation could fail at some backgrounds and hold at the empty one" without drawing the conclusion. This weakens the paper's own case needlessly.
- Overclaiming in the abstract: "All of the results … are verified in the Lean 4 proof assistant." Proposition 2, Corollary 3 and the graded view are not in the Lean sources (there is no Compensation definition anywhere). §1 and §6 are accurately scoped; the abstract is not.
- Framing in §1 is literature-heavy ("Arrhenius states, Thomas noted, Thornley restated"), and "the field has gone on citing the original statement" is asserted without checking whether Arrhenius's circulated book manuscript already uses the corrected condition (Thornley cites "forthcoming" for his WQA* form). The universal "every view in the published literature that satisfies the conditions" (§4) is backed by one example.

## Critical Issues for Revision

1. Fix the abstract's "All of the results" to match §1/§6, or add Compensation, Prop 2 and Cor 3 to the Lean development (they are short).
2. Either drop the "worse than nothing" consequence as a defining feature or, better, present the baseline-capacity variant (I + c) and use it: it shows the consequence is not what the five conditions or the capacity structure commit one to, and strengthens the reply to "the counterexample is absurd".
3. Engage the VRA*-repugnance objection from the critic's side rather than from the view's self-description; at minimum, state plainly that the "RC again" reading is how the capacity view sees its own verdict, and say what a VRC-rejecter who is not a capacity theorist should make of VRA*.
4. Correct the description of Theorem 5's premises in §5 (DA replaces WNS as well as GNE replacing NE); restore Thornley's sentence verbatim; restrict the complete-ordering equivalence remark in §4 to Thomas's vocabulary; hedge "every view in the published literature".
5. Check Arrhenius's book manuscript for the form of WQA it states and adjust the "treated as settled" paragraph accordingly.
