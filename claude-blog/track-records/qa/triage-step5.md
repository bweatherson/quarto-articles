## Decision (of the five options)
Option 3: make substantive revisions and return to step 4. The core claim (records certify only relative to something
the evaluator does not get from the record) survives; what changes is the identification of that something --
at bottom, an account of what the chances could be -- plus a new section on the metaphysics of chance (Humean vs
propensity), the corrected comparison section, a single standard of negligibility, an honest restriction on where
the strategic impossibility bites, and the Meno frame. I do not treat this as option 4 (core revision, back to the
advisor) because the thesis is a deepening of S2's, not a replacement; but the new chance-ontology claims get a
targeted verification and priority check before the revised draft goes to the second Q&A round.
