# Screening report v1: "Findable, Not Simple" -- fresh screener, 2026-10-13
Body ~5,390 (+198 abstract). 29 refs. **Verdict: NOT READY.** Contribution: "Induction needs a contingent premise that the world's regularities are feasibly findable, not that they are short, because a short pseudorandom law is statistically determinate yet computationally invisible." **Rated EXPECTED** (INTERESTING at most to someone who hasn't read Aaronson 2013 s.7 or Kearns-Valiant).
Deal-breakers: (1) priority: paper concedes "none of the ingredients is new" then claims fivefold novelty; Aaronson 2013 s.7.1 makes essentially the point (sample vs computational cost; "immense computations might be needed"); Sterkenburg and Herrmann already say Occam guarantees hide a non-simplicity condition; missing Bennett's logical depth (1988), Levin Kt / time-bounded Kolmogorov complexity, Pitt-Valiant 1988, Cherniak, bounded-rationality/logical-omniscience literature, Gold, Osherson-Stob-Weinstein, Kelly's computable-learning work. (2) abstract misstates: promises "pairs of hypotheses of equal description length" but the constructed pair H_G vs H_U differs in description length of data (about m vs k+|G| bits), so simplicity of the data DOES favour H_G; equal description length holds only for LFSR vs hash comparison. (3) formal core routine: Prop 2 is the definition of PRG; Prop 1 counting; Prop 3 (Brier bound) correct but a two-line corollary (screener checked identity and bounds; notation clash c). (4) own concession that nature may or may not contain unfindable structure makes thesis of the 'can come apart' family.
Weaknesses: reorganisation of Aaronson/Kearns-Valiant/Impagliazzo-Levin/Sterkenburg; partly straw target (the justifications are presented as sample-complexity results); s.6 slide from 'universal extrapolation easy' (Impagliazzo-Levin, Liu-Pass: polynomial-time laws, average case) to 'findability holds' assumes nature is poly-time; Nanashima 2021 'dichotomy' overstated (one direction); Aaronson quotation unverified in screener's fetch; DOIs missing; toy demo is padding.
Strengths: the credence result vs Rathmanner-Hutter 'gold standard' defence; the abduction reply (theoretical virtues rank supplied candidates but cannot supply them) is the most original passage; the OWF dichotomy observation.
Salvageable: narrowly. Drop 'new kind of underdetermination' and 'defeats the standard reply'; make the abduction argument central (virtues rank, cannot supply; supply needs structure rewarding partial guesses); position against Bennett depth and bounded rationality; state Nanashima/Liu-Pass/Impagliazzo-Levin exactly; justify poly-time nature. Expect PASS at best.
