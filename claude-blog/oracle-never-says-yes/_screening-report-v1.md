# Screening report v1: "The Oracle Never Says Yes" -- fresh screener, 2026-10-13
Body ~4,900 (+200 abstract). 21 refs. **Verdict: NOT READY.** Contribution: "Under a refutation-only method, how long a debate runs reflects the analyst's chosen vocabulary, and termination can only come from a prior restriction of that vocabulary." **Rated EXPECTED** (known proper-learning bound from equivalence queries: Angluin; Littlestone; Hanneke-Livni-Moran).
Deal-breakers: (1) paper concedes none of its theorems is new; thesis (case-counts are vocabulary-relative; stopping needs a prior class restriction) is a truism; the Gettier application is qualitative redescription (no replay; 'prediction of shape not number'), already said by Zagzebski, Weatherson 2003, the metaphilosophy literature. (2) the author saw and deferred the decisive problems: cannot say which vocabulary we are in ('a choice of vocabulary is a choice of K*'); disputed refutations; noise; no-target; 'reply to a literature' answered in a sentence. (3) internal inconsistency: long run is 'no evidence' about a fourth condition, yet a run longer than the class's bound is a 'tripwire' = evidence about the class. (4) accuracy: K* is the LARGEST minimal inconsistent set, not the certificate size (smallest certificate is often two cases); abstract's 'same number is the size of the certificate' false as stated; closest antecedent Glymour-Kelly on the Meno admittedly unread; Hanneke-Livni-Moran key bound not verified in full (screener could only see the abstract); Vlastos 1982 may be only a 4-page abstract; Chalmers 2015 attribution probably too strong; Williamson simplified; Gold only loosely relevant. (5) 21 refs; missing Lakatos 'Proofs and Refutations' (obvious antecedent), Popper, Putnam trial-and-error predicates, Williamson/Cappelen/Sosa metaphilosophy of cases, paradox of analysis, conceptual engineering.
Strengths: candid about what is taken from learning theory; proper/improper contrast (1023 vs 10 refutations) vivid and correct; tripwire framing clean (but Kelly supplies it).
Salvageable: barely; as a corollary within a paper on metaphilosophy of cases with a real defended thesis (e.g. when case verdicts underdetermine a vocabulary choice) or a worked encoding of an actual debate that produces a number.
