# Existing replies / near neighbours to Blumberg & Holguín 2025 (Phil Review 134(2): 149-201; doi 10.1215/00318108-11676103)

## Oppy & Raivars 2026, "Blumberg and Holguín on Unique Fictional Worlds", Philosophical Exchange, 1-9, doi 10.1080/29960126.2026.2712907
READ IN FULL (T&F HTML via BW's Chrome, 2026-10-23). Short note. Points:
- Doubt wonder/curiosity data: "if you know that it is in principle impossible for there to be a way of finding out whether p, then
  there is no rational way for you to be curious about whether p or to wonder whether p."
- 'Know whether' / 'sure': on a trivalent standard view, knowing p is gappy may count as knowing whether p; reject B&H's
  know-whether principle and Attitude Projection; KoF may be simply false, so B&H's 'ad hoc' charge fails.
- Wish/hope: "what I am expressing is a preference, all else being equal, for extensions of JAG according to which Bud's coin lands
  heads"; Great Expectations' revised ending example; "prop duality" flagged and deferred ("perhaps the coin that was tossed in the
  filming did land heads").
- Credence .5: "in any extension of JAG, prior to the landing of the coin, we have credence 0.5" -- agree with B&H's numbers on that
  construal.
- Pretence/sampling: "Pretend that you can uniformly sample from the nearest Oliver Twist worlds ... take away the pretences, and most
  of the attitudes stop making sense."
- Silent operator: automatic accommodation; alternate-history fictions (Hitler won WWII) as reason to resist bare truth of internal claims.
- Conclusion: "Nothing that we have said here is a direct objection to the view that, for each fiction, there is a unique world";
  "there are admissible philosophical perspectives according to which Blumberg and Holguín are mistaken in their judgments".
=> They DENY/DEFLATE the data (ears differ) and gesture at extension/pretence readings; no systematic alternative semantics; no
   account of why the pattern has exactly B&H's shape; no discriminating data.

## Khoo 2026, "Uncertainty in the absence of fact", Philosophical Studies 183(5): 1179-1215, doi 10.1007/s11098-025-02445-2
ABSTRACT + FOOTNOTES ONLY (paywalled; BW asked for PDF). Abstract: indecision without subjective uncertainty for 'reactive'
predicates (scary, disgusting); non-factual claims only have probabilities "when they are determined by probabilities about matters
of fact"; "the possibility of indecision without uncertainty is a mark of the non-factual". Footnotes: an analogous observation for
"incomplete fictions", but "in §4 I argue that a related, but slightly different, theory will be needed for incomplete fictions";
one fn: "Given Demand-Laden Probability, this predicts that someone who believes that p is not determined by the content of the
fiction F will have no non-trivial credence that p"; Flim/Jones cases: someone who falsely believes there is a fact can have a
credence; cites B&H for selection-function semantics for fictional operators.
=> Khoo DENIES B&H's credence datum for informed agents (no non-trivial credence). MUST READ §4 in full.
