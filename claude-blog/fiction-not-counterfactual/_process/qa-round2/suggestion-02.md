**Repair (d) fails for the wrong reason: done MacFarlane's way, relativism saves B&H on (1)–(4), and the story-state view needs relativism itself.**

*Where.* §5, repair (d): "(3) and (4) concern how the standing of Ann's claim changes as assessment contexts change."

*What is wrong.* That is the theorist's metalanguage. For MacFarlane the object-language "true"/"right" is monadic, and no operator, tense included, shifts the context of assessment (MacFarlane 2014; from memory; check). Assessed from 2026, "Ann was right in 1890" and "Ann is right" are both evaluated at s(B, @). So relativism predicts (3) ✓ and (4) ✗ from every context of assessment. Only the theorist says "accurate as assessed from 1890, inaccurate from 2026". McGonigal's "lucked out" is this prediction: Johnny was right all along. The PRP at each stage plus relativism passes all four tests. "Stories that have nothing to do with it" names an ugly mechanism, not a failed datum.

*Where the reshuffle resurfaces.* Relativism moves it from time into modality:
- (5) ✓ Had Doyle never written "The Gloria Scott", that would have made no difference to whether Ann was right.
- (6) ✓ Had Doyle later written that Holmes's hairs were odd in number, Ann would have been right.

For (6), the world fixed at assessment must track the counterfactual stories. Then (5) compares independent selections and fails with probability a half. If that world is rigidified to the actual stories, (5) holds but (6) fails half the time. This dilemma, not (3) and (4), defeats (d).

*What your own view must say.* "Right" is defined by {@}⊕f, but for a growing f you never say whether the stage is that of use or of assessment. §5 picks use ("the state of the stories they were made about"). Then Johnny's 1977 claim is unsupported by the 1977 state, so Jimmy's "That's true", which you cite as a datum "of the same kind", comes out wrong. A variant of (1) fails: if Ann and Carl each say "Holmes had a brother", Mycroft (1893) makes Carl right and Ann not.

So make the view assessment-sensitive: a claim is right as assessed from c iff f's state at c supports it. Refinement then guarantees what the PRP cannot: as assessment moves forward, claims go from unsettled to settled, never from right to wrong (absent revision). This is MacFarlane's semantics for future contingents, where truth at a context of assessment is truth on every history through it. Relativism plus a PRP world per stage is the fictional Thin Red Line, which MacFarlane rejects (from memory; check). Monotone against reshuffling assessment-sensitivity is your sharpest framing.
