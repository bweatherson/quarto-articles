## Main issues and fixes

1. ***Stages* clause** (03, 04, 01, 07, 08). As stipulated, the clause is inconsistent with the treatment of (D), overgenerates ("would have tossed hard"), and could be adopted by the PRP as an ANCHORING rule.
   - **Fix:** derive the comparison of versions from anaphora. A consequent with no anaphor to the actual telling ((C), (D), "would have tossed hard") is evaluated in the counterfactual telling's state. An anaphoric consequent ("differently", "still", "the other way") is evaluated by mapping each candidate of the actual telling to the nearest candidates of the counterfactual telling. That map is centred.
   - By Refinement, every candidate of the extended telling is a candidate of the shorter one, so it maps to itself. The PRP has one world per telling, and the shorter telling's world is not the extended telling's world.
   - For chance-shifting additions, use the maximal (comonotone) coupling of the two measures (07). It agrees with the identity map when the addition does not shift chances. This goes in a footnote.
   - The anaphora account predicts a "still" contrast, which separates it from ANCHORING: "would *still* have tossed hard" ✓ but "would have tossed hard" ✗. ANCHORING makes both ✓.
2. **Claim 1** (01, 02, 05).
