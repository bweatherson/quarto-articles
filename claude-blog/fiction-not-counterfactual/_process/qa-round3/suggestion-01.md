**The strengthening test doesn't refute the PRP. A Stalnakerian passes it with the selection-function twin of your own *Stages* clause, at the same cost.**

The weak step is §3, "What the PRP predicts", first bullet: "a world closest to actuality will have an ordinary one. So almost certainly s(A, @) ∉ B." Closest how? There is no actual toss to match. A hard toss costs no miracle and no mismatch of particular fact, so similarity is silent between the two. What favours the ordinary toss is π(· | A): chance, not similarity. Through your chance–selection link, π fixes a *credence* about s(A, @), not s(A, @) itself. Your tie-breaker's "fixed assignment" to the exogenous variables is free here, because @ assigns *JAG*'s variables no values.

So let selection vary with context, as Stalnaker always said it does (B&H 185 grant this):

> ANCHORING. When a compound mentions two tellings with P-Fics A ⊇ B, the operative selection function chooses the world of A inside B.

Stalnaker's coherence condition (if s(A) ∈ B and s(B) ∈ A, then s(A) = s(B); from memory, check) then gives s(A) = s(B). Verdicts: (A) ✓: the in-story counterfactual runs from s(B), with your causal tie-breaker intact. (B) ✗: same world, same coin. (C) ✓: s(B∩h) lies in h. (D) ✓: credence π(h | B) = .5. Your third bullet misses this: I anchor the selection, not the world, so (C) survives. Adding a detail is then strengthening by what already holds at the selected world, not intervention.

Ad hoc? It is *Stages* in selection dress. Refinement is to your state what coherence is to my selection function. *Stages* also shows what drives the (B) ✗ intuition. It supports "If the writers had left the special out, Bud would still have tossed the coin unusually hard." Likewise, "Had *NCIS: LA* never brought them back, Mac would still have won the toss." That is the realist's thought that tellings report facts and don't make them. Your (B) verdict expresses my intuition, not yours.

**Concessions.** The test refutes the PRP as B&H state it, with selection that ignores context. They owe ANCHORING. ANCHORING also makes two-telling compounds assessment-sensitive. But you pay that cost too (§4, *Truth*, *Stages*), and you grant it to B&H for temporal talk (§3, last paragraph).

**What to do.** Either find a case where *Stages* and ANCHORING come apart, or weaken claim 1 to "the PRP needs ANCHORING". If you weaken it, §5's ledger changes. The single-world rival becomes the anchored PRP, which keeps the unification with counterfactuals, and your case shrinks to parsimony against an arbitrariness B&H already accept (188).
