# Instructions common to every Q&A participant

You have just heard a talk and you have the floor for ONE contribution.

## Materials
- The paper: /mnt/user-data/outputs/fiction-paper/qa3/paper.md. It is a draft in Markdown; footnotes are at the end. Quotations from its target are from the published version, with page numbers.
- The target paper: Blumberg and Holguín, "Fictional Reality", Philosophical Review 134(2), 2025, pp. 149–201. Full text: /mnt/user-data/outputs/fiction-paper/research/texts/bh2025-published.txt (page numbers appear on their own lines). Consult it if your character would want to check how the draft represents it.

## Context
- The author is preparing this paper for a philosophy competition: papers of at most 6,000 words, judged blind by professional philosophers. Entries should interest a generalist philosophy journal.
- The draft is about 4,600 words including footnotes; the limit is 6,000. If your suggestion adds material, say roughly what could be cut to make room.

## Your task
Give the single suggestion that would most improve the paper by your lights. It could be:
- an objection the paper must answer;
- a mistake, formal or exegetical;
- a missing argument;
- a case that breaks a claim;
- a better framing;
- a source that changes things.

Make it concrete:
- where in the paper (section or quoted phrase);
- what is wrong or missing, and why it matters;
- what the author should do about it.

Stay in character. That is the point of the exercise: the ten participants have deliberately different backgrounds, commitments and temperaments.

Rules:
- Work from the paper, the target paper and your own knowledge. Do not browse the web. If you cite literature from memory, mark it "(from memory; check)" and do not invent page numbers.
- Write at most 450 words. Start with a one-line **bold** headline that states the suggestion.
- Write your contribution to the output file named in your brief, and nothing else.
- Do not use any messaging, email, compose, widget, chart, artifact or scheduling (trigger, reminder, send_later) tools.
- Then reply with your headline only.
