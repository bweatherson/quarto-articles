**Your special is chance-neutral, the one case where Stages' same-world coupling is credally innocent: add a chance-shifting special and replace Stages with a minimal coupling.**

*Where.* §4 *Stages* and the Refinement parenthesis; §3's closing generalisation; §5's "the data tie".

*Problem.* Bud's hard toss changes a cause of the outcome but not its chance: C⊕A(heads) = C⊕B(heads) = .5. That is why *Stages* (T_Xφ takes the value |φ|(v) at each world v of s⊕Y) yields (B) ✗ and (D) ✓ together. It is a diagonal coupling of the outcome under the two tellings, and a diagonal coupling exists only when the marginals coincide.

Now let the special reveal a trick giving heads nine times in ten. However the outcome in the no-special story (.5 heads) is jointly distributed with the outcome in the extended story (.9), the two must disagree with probability at least .4 (Fréchet: P(X≠Y) ≥ |P(X)−P(Y)|). Stages avoids this only by letting the claim about the poorer version inherit the richer version's marginal. So (D′), "It's about even odds that, if the writers had left the special out, the coin would have landed heads", comes out .9, while the same claim outside the compound gets .5. Defend that prediction or amend Stages. It also falsifies "adding to the story ... moves none": here it moves credence.

The defaults view fares worse. One default per question forces equal credence that each version's world has heads, but B&H's own fair and biased versions of JAG (169) need .5 and 2/3. "The data tie" then holds only if defaults are drawn jointly by quantile, a coupling in disguise.

*What to do.*
1. Replace Stages' same-world clause with the maximal (comonotone) coupling of C⊕X and C⊕Y. It is the diagonal when the addition is chance-neutral, so your verdicts stand. Otherwise it gives (D′) = .5 and disagreement of exactly .4.
2. Restate §3's moral. Adding a detail moves the outcome only as far as the new chances require (conditioning). Strengthening an antecedent resamples whatever depends on the cause (intervention). The chance–selection link fixes only marginals, so the PRP's disagreement is set by the structural equations: at least .4, typically more. "About half the time" follows from (A) via the shared tie-breaker; say so, or state it as cr(B) = cr(A).
3. Add the chance-shifting special to the experimental footnote. (D′) is a bare marginal judgement, free of cross-world coupling and of the anaphora in "the other way".
