# Pretest question (no thesis)

A speaker has credences and is asked a series of questions in conversation. Each answer is a guess, in the sense of the recent guessing literature: Holguín 2022; Dorst & Mandelkern 2022, "Good Guesses"; Goodman & Holguín 2023; Fang 2026; Teague 2024; Pearson 2025. Consider these exchanges. Judgements of naturalness are given.

**D1 (Latif). Credences: Yale .30, Harvard .28, Stanford .22, NYU .20.**
- A: Will Latif go to Yale? B: No, I don't think so.
- A: So where do you think he'll go? B: Harvard, I'd guess. (natural)

**D2 (Clue). Credences: butler-knife .40, butler-gun .15, gardener-gun .45.**
- A: Who did it? B: The butler.
- A: With what? B: The knife, then. (natural; "the gun" sounds off here, though P(gun) = .60)

**D3 (Raffle line). Ticket A has .40; sixty other tickets have .01 each.**
- A: Will A win? B: No, I don't think so.
- A: Will #1 win? B: No.
- … and so on, through:
- A: Will #60 win? B: No, probably not. (natural, unmarked, even though if the earlier "no"s are all true then #60 wins)

**D4 (the same line, then).**
- A: So who do you think will win? B: Well, A, I suppose. I know I said no. (natural)
- B: #60. (absurd, though it is the only answer consistent with all B's earlier answers)

**D5 (Latif, all "no"s).** B says "No" to Yale, to Harvard, to Stanford and to NYU in turn. Then:
- A: So where? B: Yale, I suppose. I know I said no. (natural)

**D6 (Holguín 2022 §9.2).** After answering "London" to "Which city?", answering "Germany" to "Which country?" sounds bad.

**D7 (Fang 2026).** After guessing "A" to race 1 and "D or E" to race 2, reporting "I think A will win the first race and D or E the second" sounds fine. Yet "(A,D) or (A,E)" is not a filtered answer to the joint question.

**The questions:**
- What do a speaker's guesses commit her to in later turns?
- When must later answers cohere with earlier guesses, and when may (or must) earlier guesses be given up?
- What theory best explains D1–D7, including D3–D5?
- What would a competent philosopher of language or epistemologist, who knows this literature, propose?
