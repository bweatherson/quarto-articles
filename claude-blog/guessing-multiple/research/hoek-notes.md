- Topic: minimal (Cherniak) rationality for FULL belief; beliefs are answers to questions ("quizpositions" <Q, A>).
- Harmonic Parts (p. 16 of proof): "If a rational agent has beliefs about Q, then they have matching beliefs about every
  part R of Q: that is to say, they believe all and only those quizpositions about R that are entailed by their beliefs
  about Q." Plus Non-Absurdity. Consequences: Closure under Parthood, Partial Adjunction, Minimal Consistency.
- Fragmentation critique (p. 12): adding pairwise consistency among fragments "seems rather ad hoc".
- fn 9: "Being a fragmentation theorist, Yalcin posits no such constraints" (coherence across questions); on Yalcin's
  account "one can believe A ∧ B in answer to Q ∧ R without believing A in answer to Q."
- s VI Tautological belief updates (p. 28ff): B + A_Q = smallest harmonic set containing B ∪ {A_Q}. Posing a new
  question Q = updating with the tautologous answer Q_Q. Example: Cob believes Hob is 5'5" (H) and Nob 5'6" (N);
  "Suppose Cob now forms a view on the conjunctive question HN for the first time ... this tautological update
  effectively amounts to adjoining Cob's views on H and N." Deductive inquiry "as a question-guided endeavour"
  (Meno; cf. Pérez Carballo 2016, Friedman 2017).
- No probabilities, no weak belief, no guesses (one occurrence of 'guess'), no order-dependence: updates are
  monotone (smallest harmonic superset), so posing questions in different orders gives the same result.

Relevance: Hoek is the nearest antecedent for (i) coherence constraints across questions (Harmonic Parts ~ Teague's
WTP; fn 24 of Teague) and (ii) a DYNAMIC, question-by-question model of belief formation in which posing the
conjunctive question adjoins earlier views (structurally like Independent Closure). Our view differs: weak belief /
guessing under uncertainty, each new guess made from credence conditional on earlier guesses; harmony holds within a
line but not across lines; order matters. Must cite and state the difference precisely.
